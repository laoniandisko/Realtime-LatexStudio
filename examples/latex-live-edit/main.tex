\section{Paragraph editing}
A coherent scene requires stable geometry and consistent appearance. This entire paragraph can be revised from the preview. Adding sentences updates line wrapping and background pagination.

A \textbf{formatted paragraph} retains its commands. Its inline formulas are $E=mc^2$ and \(\alpha+\beta\). Use the LaTeX mode or a formula button to edit them.
\section{Display equations}
The energy is defined by Equation~\ref{eq:energy}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:energy}
  E = mc^2.
\end{equation}
The equation label and numbering stay outside the edited expression.

Multiple aligned rows can be edited together:
\begin{align}
  a + b &= c, \\
  x^2 + y^2 &= z^2.
\end{align}
